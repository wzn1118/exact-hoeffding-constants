\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains the full proofs of the fixed-sample success-count extremality and exact imbalance response, analytic contraction on compact success-proportion intervals, and auxiliary pointwise-curvature examples. The main paper proves the parity-dependent exact Hoeffding constants independently of these refinements. The symbols and equation references used here are those of the accompanying manuscript.
\end{abstract}
