# Exact Hoeffding Constants for Finite Exchangeable Sequences

**Zinan Wang** · Revised 11 October 2026

**Copyright (c) 2026 Zinan Wang. All rights reserved.** See [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md).

[Read the manuscript](manuscript.pdf) · [Single-file submission source](submission/manuscript.tex) · [Chinese guide](README_zh.md) · [Bibliography](refs/README_zh.md)

This paper determines the optimal Hoeffding constant for centered linear contrasts of bounded exchangeable sequences of every finite length:
[
C_N^\star=\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac{N}{N-1},&N\text{ even},\\[4pt]
\displaystyle\frac{1}{(N-1)\operatorname{artanh}(1/N)},&N\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
]

For even populations the constant is the largest normalized variance. For odd populations the sharp coefficient is strictly larger, with an extremizer at an explicit nonzero Laplace tilt.

The proofs include the two-level exchangeability reduction, a sharp hypergeometric tilted-variance comparison, and a **parity-uniform scaled Bernoulli Laplace envelope** in its canonical parameter range. The manuscript determines the odd extremizers and gives fourth-order expansions of optimized variance proxies.
