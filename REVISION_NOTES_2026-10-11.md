# Revision summary — 11 October 2026

This is the sole current publication edition of *Exact Hoeffding Constants for Finite Exchangeable Sequences* by Zinan Wang.

The editorial revision foregrounds the sharp parity-dependent constants and moves the Lee–Kim two-level reduction ahead of the polynomial analysis. The Pochhammer rising/falling conventions are made explicit. The scaled Bernoulli Laplace envelope extends to the even canonical range, with equality conditions treated separately at balanced margins.

The cumulant expansions and secondary curvature comparisons are organized into technical appendices. The main sharp constants, extremal classification, and optimized asymptotics are retained.

The 33-page manuscript PDF on `main` is built from `source/manuscript.tex`. The standalone submission source is regenerated from the modular source. The publication workflow refreshes the PDF and checksums when manuscript code changes.
