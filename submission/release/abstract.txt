We determine the least constant in the Hoeffding-type exponential-moment inequality for arbitrary centered linear contrasts of bounded exchangeable sequences of length $N$. The sharp coefficient is $C_N^\star=N/(N-1)$ for even $N$ and $C_N^\star=2/[(N-1)\log((N+1)/(N-1))]$ for odd $N$. The even coefficient coincides with the maximal normalized variance. In odd populations the optimal Laplace parameter is nonzero, and the sharp coefficient strictly exceeds the variance benchmark. We prove an all-tilt hypergeometric variance bound and a parity-uniform mean-dependent Laplace envelope, and determine all odd-population extremizers. Further results give fourth-order optimal-proxy expansions, fixed-sample success-count extremality, and analytic contraction on compact success-proportion intervals.
